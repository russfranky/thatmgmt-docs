# Lock a quote

`GET /v1/domains/quote` quotes a registration without purchasing. The quote is the locked price: wholesale cost plus the flat 10% platform cut, bound into one payable total.

```bash
curl -H "Authorization: Bearer $TMGMT_API_KEY" \
  "https://api.thatmgmt.com/v1/domains/quote?domain=example.com&period=1"
```

## Parameters

| Name | Required | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `domain` | yes | Fully qualified domain name. |
| `period` | no | Registration period in years (default 1). |

## What the quote gives you

- The verified payable total your approval and budget bind to.
- No provider quote tokens: they are never returned.
- No purchase: quoting never spends anything and never registers the domain.

Locked quotes fail closed. If the supplier-cost provenance is stale or unverified, the quote fails instead of returning a guessed price.

## Responses

`200` returns the locked quote. `401` means the key is missing or invalid. `503` means the route composition is not wired.
